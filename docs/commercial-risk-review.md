# Commercial risk review — local demo slice

Not deployed. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261031085511_commercial_risk_reviews.sql` before enabling backend `ENABLE_COMMERCIAL_REVIEW=true` and frontend build variable `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_COMMERCIAL_REVIEW=true`. Both flags default off. No new model or storage provider is required. Reconcile existing remote migration history before any bulk migration push.

Uses existing AI-extracted pricing, minimum-commitment and refund fields. Recomputes literal attribution against hash-verified retained page text. Unique source matches preserve exact wording, qualifications and page numbers; repeated, missing and paraphrased clauses remain not assessed. Keyword signals suggest manual review; they do not determine commercial acceptability, severity, absence, governing amendments or completeness. This is a bounded review assistant, not a full procurement playbook engine. Severity deliberately remains unassessed.

GET/PATCH `/contracts/{id}/commercial-review` derive organization from authentication. PATCH is authenticated and authorized before bounded body parsing. A reason is mandatory. Reviews persist by source-bound finding fingerprint, so source changes do not silently inherit confirmation. A service-only invoker RPC locks the owned active contract, checks actor membership, compares revision, and atomically writes current state plus an audit event. Confirm, dismiss and needs-context do not update the renewal decision or trigger reminders. Old reviews remain private historical evidence until contract deletion; source changes remove them from current output. All workspace members can review, matching the existing membership model; approval roles are not introduced.

Reports include exact page citations and explicit confirmed/unreviewed/context/dismissed status, including unreviewed items transparently. Internal reasons and reviewer identities are excluded. Exports are read-only. Defaults preserve the previous report when this feature is off. With it on, a source/database verification failure fails the export rather than silently claiming review completion. Original PDF files are not stored; highlights and section citations are outside this slice.

The UI asks reviewers to inspect surrounding terms/amendments. It does not assign high/low risk from a keyword, claim usage waste, or infer refund remedies beyond the quote. The three saved clauses are the coverage boundary. Missing referenced documents and mixed-scan completeness are not automatically resolved. Existing chat can connect a legacy record's original PDF once. No new billing, quotas or paid-plan promises are added.

Local checks and generated fictional report/screenshots are in `tmp/commercial-review/`. Regression tests include `test_contract_risks.py` and `test_contract_risks_postgres.py`; the latter uses the existing loopback-only disposable `renewai_reminder_test` guard. Never point it at a customer or production database.

## Policy severity extension — local only, 31 October 2026

The original three-category review was released at16abe71. This new extension is **not deployed**. It introduces `renewai-demo-procurement-v1`, a server-owned, deterministic demo policy selected with Raj's permission to choose initial thresholds. These are product defaults for reviewing cited terms, not industry standards, legal conclusions, customer-approved policy or measured accuracy.

Pricing caps per renewal: ≤5% Low; >5% through10% Medium; >10% High. Minimum duration: ≤12months Low;13–24months Medium; >24months High (whole years convert to12months). Refunds: explicit convenience termination with pro-rata unused prepaid fee refund Low; explicit non-refundability with a provider-breach exception Medium; explicit non-refundability **without exceptions** High. Bare non-refundable wording, unspecified quantities, unresolved references, competing rates, unsupported grammar and qualifications remain Not assessed. Literal grounding is mandatory for every rating. Low is not an all-clear for the agreement; all ratings are provisional and cover only the quoted candidate.

`backend/risk_policy.py` owns the policy metadata, thresholds and bounded grammar; `severity_reason` explains each rating or abstention. UI and PDF include the policy name, rule summary, qualification, rating and reason, with original citations. No LLM selects severity. No date calculations, reviewed terms, reminder operations, billing/entitlements, migrations or provider configuration change.

The `commercial-review-v2` fingerprint includes the complete policy snapshot plus source/field identity. Earlier confirmations are not reused for the new ratings; old audit records remain untouched. Future threshold or policy changes invalidate that review identity too. A policy upgrade requires renewed human review. Reports export the current named policy rather than claiming to reconstruct historical ratings.

Tests cover boundary percentages/durations, years, unsupported terms/negation, refund exceptions, ambiguous sources, policy identity changes, old confirmation isolation, source quotation and report privacy. Local browser/API checks use only fictional source text and simulated Auth/storage. No new PostgreSQL checks are necessary for this inference/presentation-only change; existing transaction semantics are unchanged. Further procurement-reviewed corpus evaluation is required before promising broad interpretation or enterprise-calibrated severity.
